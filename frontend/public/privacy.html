M2O

Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

M2O turns meeting transcripts into reviewed outcomes and, when you approve it, sends them to tools you connect. This page says what M2O stores, where, for how long, and what leaves it. It covers this site: the public demo and invited private accounts.

The public demo

"Explore the isolated demo" starts a temporary session with its own workspaces and synthetic example meetings. The demo accepts no text, files or recordings of your own and sends nothing to outside tools. What you do in it (reviews, plans) is kept with the session, which expires after two hours and is then deleted automatically.

Invited private accounts

Accounts exist only by invitation. For an account M2O stores:

  • your email address, name and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself);
  • the meetings you add: transcripts, the outcomes found in them, your reviews and your personal plan;
  • an audit history of changes and deliveries, so a workspace can see who did what;
  • for each tool you connect (Jira, Slack, LinkedIn), the access the tool granted, encrypted, plus the destination you chose.

What leaves M2O

Nothing is sent to a connected tool until you approve the exact preview of what will be sent. Then only that content goes to that tool, under your connection. M2O does not sell data, show advertising or use your content to train models. Outcomes are found by rules running on this server; no outside AI service reads your transcripts.

Where it is kept

The site runs on Render and its database on Neon, both in the United States (Virginia). Both providers process the data only to host this service.

How long, and your choices

Private data stays until you delete it. In the app, Settings → Privacy lets you export your account or a workspace and delete them. Disconnecting a tool removes its stored access; content already delivered to that tool stays there under the tool's own rules. Deleted data may remain in the database provider's short-term recovery history for a limited time before it is gone.
